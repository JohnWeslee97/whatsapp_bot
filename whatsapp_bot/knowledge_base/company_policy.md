# ACME Corporation - Company FAQ & Grievance Policy

## 1. Operating Hours
Our office hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST. Support is closed on public holidays.

## 2. Grievance Submission Procedure
To submit a grievance or complaint:
1. Complete the Grievance Redressal Form available on the employee/customer portal.
2. Attach valid Government ID (Aadhaar / Passport) and relevant transaction or issue reference numbers.
3. Submit the ticket. A unique Grievance Tracking ID (e.g. GRV-2026-XXXX) will be generated.
4. Resolution time is typically 3 to 5 business days.

- Annual leave policy: Employees get 15 days of paid annual leave per year.
- Sick leave policy: Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
- Maternity & Paternity Leave: Available as per standard company policy and government regulations.
